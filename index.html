FAQ

Questions card holders ask

Can I use my GPC with a Korean vendor?

Yes. We are SAM registered and provide Form 889 and the VAT-exemption purchase certificate with every quote.

Do you deliver outside Humphreys and Osan?

Yes. We deliver to U.S. installations in every area, Area I through Area VI, including Camp Casey, Yongsan, Daegu, Busan and Kunsan.

Can you quote an item you don't list?

Yes. Send a part number, model or link and we will source it in Korea or import it.

Can you buy furniture through GSA?

Yes. We hold GSA MAS contract 47QSMS24D001L for office furniture.

How do I order USFK supplies online with a GPC?

Search the catalog, add items to your quote list and send it. We reply by email with a VAT-exempt quote, Form 889 and the purchase certificate, then deliver to your unit anywhere in Korea.

Do you invoice through WAWF?

Yes. For contract orders we submit the DD250 and invoice in WAWF.

What paperwork comes with a GPC quote?

An itemized quote, Form 889 and the USFK VAT-exemption purchase certificate for the Korean tax office.
